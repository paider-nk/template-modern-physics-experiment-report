# Worked examples: four reports produced with this template

These four reports were produced in one continuous working session and converged on a
single LaTeX pipeline. Use them as evidence for *how* the template behaves in practice:
what the source material looked like, which structural and figure decisions were made,
what broke, and how the result was verified. `assets/latex/report-template.tex` is the
preamble extracted from them; `assets/latex/figure-snippets.tex` holds the reusable
figure patterns; `references/latex-layout-spec.md` holds the numbers and the build checks.

All four share the same skeleton: title block → 摘要/关键词 → 实验背景/目的 → 实验原理 →
实验内容与步骤 → 实验装置 → 结果与数据处理 → 误差分析 → 思考题 → 结论 → 参考文献
(plus an English title/abstract block where the reference required one).

## 1. 9.9 锁相放大器 (lock-in amplifier) — 22 pages

**Source.** A Word document with 35 embedded images plus a supplied template. The first
attempt rewrote it as OOXML with Word COM; that route was abandoned after repeated
“file may be corrupt” failures and because equation numbers could not be right-aligned
inside OMML paragraphs. The report was rewritten from scratch in LaTeX, keeping the
Word file only as a content source.

**Structure.** 摘要 → 1 实验背景 → 2 实验原理 (2.1 相关检测与相位器; 2.2 同步积分器的
电路方程与推导; 2.3 噪声来源、统计特性与抑制机制) → 3 实验内容 → 4 实验装置 → 5 实验数据
及分析 (5.1–5.8) → 6 结论与体会 → 7 思考题 → 8 参考文献 → 附录表 A1–A4 → English block.
28 numbered equations; the appendix tables hold the raw readings so §5 can stay analytical.

**Figures.** Principle diagrams (correlator, synchronous integrator) were drawn in TikZ
because the measurements table and the derivation both refer to their terminal names.
The two wiring diagrams were finally taken as the user's own images: cropping only the
built-in title and the “实心点表示连接” note, then `\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]`,
because every hand-drawn attempt put labels on top of boxes. Measured waveforms, phase
curves and harmonic-response plots are pgfplots on the actual data.

**Data.** Correlator: `U(φ)=A cos(φ−φ₀)+C` with A = 1.0360 V, φ₀ = 8.02°, C = −0.0164 V,
R² = 0.9999. Synchronous integrator: R² = 0.9820, A = 0.0883 V, φ₀ = −81.22°,
C = 0.1127 V. Both fits are discussed parameter by parameter — the offset φ₀ is attributed
to cable delay and instrument zero, the constant C to amplifier offset.

**What broke.** `\ne` collided with a LaTeX primitive; a TikZ `scope` with `shift=` skewed
every `\draw plot` inside it; a `[H]` figure pushed the page break so that an explanatory
sentence fell off the page (found by scanning the page render line by line).

**Verification.** Page count, zero log errors, and a rendered page for every figure page;
the final PDF was copied to the desktop folder as well as the project folder.

## 2. 4.5 电光调制器特性的测试 (electro-optic modulator) — 15 pages

**Source.** A Word report whose 35 images were mostly figures generated by an earlier
script: they had missing-glyph boxes (“P□”, “LiNbO□”, “XD”) and inconsistent line weights.
Decision: redraw every schematic in TikZ and keep only the 12 genuine photographs
(oscilloscope screen, apparatus, waveform captures). The pixel data was re-read from
`直流电光强关系数据记录+.xlsx` (23 points) rather than transcribed from the old prose.

**Structure.** 2 实验原理 builds the chain in the order a reader needs it: 2.1 介电响应与
折射率椭球 → 2.2 线性电光系数张量的对称性与 Voigt 约化 (with the 6×3 matrix) → 2.3
LiNbO₃ 的电光矩阵与折射率椭球 → 2.4 半波电压 → 2.5 横向与纵向电光调制结构 → 2.6 偏振干涉与
余弦型传输特性 → 2.7 静态消光比与交流调制. Each later section reuses an earlier equation
number, so the derivation is genuinely one chain rather than seven summaries.

**Figures.** 图 1 折射率椭球 (two panels: before/after field, axis rotation at 45°) →
图 2/3 纵向/横向结构 → 图 4 余弦传输曲线 → 图 5 四种工作点 (top: transmission curve with
four marked points; bottom: four output waveforms, 2ω / same-frequency / distorted) →
图 6/7/8 measurement paths and the AC/DC/quarter-wave wiring → 图 9/10 apparatus →
图 11 optical-path photo → 图 12 I–V data with the fit → 图 13 four waveform photos →
表 2 the photo matrix as a coordinate system → 表 3 the ideal waveform classification.

**Data.** Vπ = 558.07 V; fit `I(V)=a+b cos(πV/Vπ)+c sin(πV/Vπ)` with a = 2614.1,
b = −2513.4, c = 823.2, R² = 0.99518, RMSE = 129.2; γ₂₂ = 5.16×10⁻¹² m/V;
M_s = 5150/23 = 223.91, M_d = 5400/26 = 207.69; modulation depth m = π·60/Vπ ≈ 0.338.

**What broke.**
- pgfplots evaluates `cos`/`sin` in degrees. Writing m = 2.2 rad into
  `0.5*(1-cos(90+2.2*sin(x)))` gives an almost flat line; the depth must be entered as
  126° (68.8°, 20.1° for 1.2 rad and 0.35 rad).
- Padding all waveform photos onto a 6:5 canvas made them look wide and short next to
  the previous version; 4:5 with equal framing restored the intended look, and the
  table cells were resized to match (2.34 cm → 1.62 cm).
- 表 2 (positions × bias voltage, each cell a photo) did not work as a `tabular`; it was
  rebuilt as a TikZ coordinate system with the photos placed at (x, y) data points.
- Six schematic figures overflowed the text block; giving the `tikzpicture`
  `x=0.86cm,y=1cm` compresses them horizontally without shrinking the font, which
  `\resizebox` cannot do.

**Verification.** Every figure page rendered and inspected; the two-photo apparatus figure
was merged into one double-panel figure when the two separate ones left a half-empty page.

## 3. 电子自旋共振实验及 DPPH 的 g 因子测量 — 16 pages

**Source.** A 19-page 12 pt PDF plus its `.tex` and an assets directory of 20 images and
8 TikZ fragments. The original text was dense with production notes (“（按系统说明图三
重绘）”, “（保留原图一）”) and defensive sentences (“原始资料未给出…不虚构数值误差棒”).
The revision deleted every one of them and kept the physics untouched.

**Structure.** 2 实验原理: 2.1 轨道磁矩、自旋磁矩与朗德因子 (with the full `g_J` reduction)
→ 2.2 塞曼分裂、跃迁选择定则与共振条件 → 2.3 扫场方法与提高灵敏度的方法 → 2.4 微波器件、
魔 T 电桥与谐振腔 → 2.5 扫场显示、共振场和微波频率的测量. 5 结果 → 6 误差 → 7 思考题
(four graded questions, each answered in layers) → 8 实验总结与应用展望 → 9 参考文献.

**Figures.** 图 1 系统工作原理 (block chain with the X/Y routing drawn around the detector
box, because a straight line to the oscilloscope ran through the 检波器 label) → 图 2 魔 T
→ 图 3 腔体与驻波分布 → 图 4 时间扫描定中心 → 图 5 五类吸收/色散信号 → 图 6 调相重合
→ 图 7 电网波形质量 → 图 9 系统照片 → 图 10 装置双联照 → 图 11 示波器波形 → 图 13 DPPH
结构 (the Baidu Baike watermark was painted out, never kept).

**Data.** ν = 9370 MHz nominal, 9367 MHz measured; B₀ = 0.336 T; g = hν/(μ_B B₀) = 1.9918
against a literature 2.0036, δ ≈ 0.59 %, traced to field calibration and centre finding.
The `g ≈ 2.0023` discussion is split into three explicit layers (Dirac value, QED anomaly,
molecular correction), with the molecular part expressed as Δg from spin-orbit mixing.

**What broke.** `\captionsetup{font={\zihao{-5}}}` raises `\zihao{-5} undefined` — the
caption package expands the argument in a context where the ctex size command is not
defined, so it must go through `\DeclareCaptionFont`. The reference list split 3/2 across
two pages until `\needspace{11\baselineskip}` was placed before the section.

## 4. 荧光光谱的测量与分析 (fluorescence spectroscopy) — 11 pages

**Source.** A 19-page, 5.2 MB PDF plus `.tex` and 20 JPEGs (4.9 MB). Images were resampled
to their display width and re-encoded (q ≈ 82), bringing the finished PDF to 1.7 MB.
Reference list reduced from the original to three entries, with every in-text marker
renumbered in the same pass.

**Structure.** 2 实验原理: 2.1 分子荧光及激发、发射光谱 → 2.2 荧光强度、量子产额与浓度关系
(quantum yield, Beer–Lambert, the `1−10^{−εbc}` factor) → 2.3 主要影响因素 → 2.4 散射背景和
光栅级次 → 2.5 狭缝宽度与光谱测量. 4 实验内容 arranged strictly by the two days and six
tasks (Day 1: best wavelengths, Rayleigh peak, concentration series; Day 2: slits, scatter
re-check, second-order ghost). 5 结果 → 6 误差 → 7 思考题 → 8 总结.

**Figures.** 图 1 Jablonski diagram (S₀/S₁/T₁ bands, vibrational levels, five vertical
arrows with labels on `pos=`, so nothing overlaps) → 图 2/3 apparatus and reagents →
图 4 excitation/emission peak-pick → 图 5 blank vs sample → 图 6 five concentration
spectra in 2+2+1 → 图 7 slit-width set in 2+2+1 → 图 8 second-order ghost in 2+1 →
图 9 concentration–intensity relationship.

**Data.** λ_ex ≈ 468 nm, λ_em ≈ 524 nm, Δλ ≈ 56 nm. Peak intensity at 5/10/15/20/25
μg mL⁻¹: 130.639 / 223.871 / 288.471 / 311.321 / 321.311, with interval increments
93.232 / 64.600 / 22.850 / 9.990. Slit 5-5 / 10-5 / 5-10 / 10-10 nm →
223.871 / 486.897 / 252.156 / 1015.643, i.e. ×2.18 / ×1.13 / ×4.54 against the 5-5
baseline, compared honestly with the `I ∝ W_ex W_em` throughput expectation.

**What broke.** The concentration figure had carried an exponential “saturation fit” whose
parameters matched neither the low- nor the high-concentration points; it was replaced by
a smooth join of the five measured points and the caption stopped calling it a fit.
`[H]` on the two apparatus figures left a half-page blank; `[htbp]` let the text fill the
page and moved the photos to a clean plate. The Jablonski diagram needed three iterations
before every label sat in empty space.

## Shared lessons

1. **The template is the contract.** Every report used the same class options, margins,
   heading sizes, caption font, header and footer. Nothing was tuned per report.
2. **Redraw schematics, keep photographs.** Schematic figures from an old Word pipeline
   carry broken glyphs and inconsistent weights; redrawing in TikZ takes minutes and looks
   like the rest of the document. Photographs are evidence and are never redrawn.
3. **Figure sizing is decided by role, not by file size.** See the table in
   `references/latex-layout-spec.md` §4. Pre-processing for equal aspect ratio happens in
   Pillow, before typesetting.
4. **Every number comes from the data.** Fits, R², ratios and derived constants were all
   recomputed from the workbook or the recorded readings; no value was carried over from
   prose that could not be re-derived.
5. **Delete the scaffolding from the prose.** No production notes, no “the source does not
   say…”, no meta-commentary about what the report is not doing. State what was measured,
   how it was reduced, and what limits the result.
6. **Look at the rendered pages.** All four reports were reviewed page by page from PNG
   renders; every layout defect listed above was found that way, not in the log.
